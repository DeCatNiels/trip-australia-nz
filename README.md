# Australia + New Zealand trip ideas

A photo board of things to do in Australia and New Zealand (December). Star the ideas you like; click a photo to browse more photos of that activity. A "Before we go" checklist lists what to sort out before the trip.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No server or build step needed.

- Click a photo to open the viewer. Use the arrows, the arrow keys, swipe, or the thumbnails to move between photos.
- ★ saves an idea to your list (stored in your own browser only).
- "Show starred only" filters the board down to your picks.
- The label buttons under it (Hiking, Nature & wildlife, Sights, …) show one label at a time; click again to clear. They combine with the other filters. Each activity's `tags` in `activities.js` is a list of label keys; the keys and their names are in `TAGS` in `app.js`, colours in `style.css`.
- "Share my picks" copies a link with your stars in it (`#picks=...`). Whoever opens it sees a "Friend ✓" badge on your picks, a count of picks in common, and a "Both picked" filter. A newer link replaces the previous one. Share the hosted URL, not a local `file://` one.
- The map shows every place as a dot (blue Australia, teal New Zealand, yellow when starred). Click a dot, then "Show the card" to jump to it. Each activity's `coords` in `activities.js` is `[latitude, longitude]`; food lists have none.
- "Before we go" (above the board) opens the checklist of things to check before leaving: passports and entry (eVisitor, NZeTA), biosecurity, health, insurance, money and cards, driving, phone and power. Tick items off as you go; ticks are saved in your own browser only. Rules and fees change, so check the linked official sites close to booking.

## Files

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `activities.js` | The stops and activities. Edit this to add, change or remove ideas. |
| `checklist.js` | The "Before we go" checklist: documents, biosecurity, health, insurance, money, driving, phone. Each item has a `text`, a `detail` and an optional `link` to the official site. Renaming an item's `text` or its group's `title` clears its tick. |
| `photos.js` | Links to the photos (hosted on Wikimedia and Flickr) and their credits. Generated by `fetch_photos.py`. |
| `fetch_photos.py` | Finds photos on Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons and Openverse (openly licensed photos, mostly Flickr). |
| `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js` | The page. |

## Adding or fixing photos

Needs Python 3, nothing else. The page loads the photos straight from Wikimedia and Flickr, so it needs an internet connection.

```sh
python3 fetch_photos.py                       # fetch photos for activities that have none yet
python3 fetch_photos.py --force "Roys Peak"   # refetch one activity
python3 fetch_photos.py --force               # refetch everything
python3 fetch_photos.py --source openverse    # only one source (commons or openverse)
python3 fetch_photos.py --count 8             # more photos per source (default 6)
```

Each activity in `activities.js` controls its photos with:

- `wiki`: Wikipedia article whose main image becomes the cover photo.
- `search`: search terms for the other photos, used on both sources.
- `exclude`: photos to hide and never fetch again, by their `id` in `photos.js`, e.g. `["File:Some bad photo.jpg", "openverse:1b2c..."]`. The viewer's "source" link opens a photo's page if you need to identify it.
- `pin`: Commons file titles to always show first, in that order.
- `photosOf`: for a card that merges others, the old cards (`"stop:name"`) whose photos it shows. These cards are not fetched.

The script never deletes entries. Photos of removed or merged cards stay in `photos.js`, unused.

After changing any of these, run the script with `--force "<activity name>"`.

A stop with `"kind": "food"` is a list of things to try rather than a place on the route: it gets a 🍴 instead of a stop number. Any stop can have an `intro`, a short paragraph shown under its heading.
